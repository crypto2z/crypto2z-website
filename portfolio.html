// investment strategy
core approach
Dollar Cost Averaging
Automated buys 3× per week regardless of price. Small fixed amounts remove emotion from entry timing and smooth out volatility over time.
dip buying
Volatility-Based Extra Buys
When a coin drops beyond its historical dip threshold (e.g. BTC −7.5%, AVAX −13.5%), the bot triggers an additional buy on top of the regular DCA.
risk management
Trailing Stop System
Non-BTC coins trigger a 50% position sell if they drop 30% from their 30-day high, checked daily at 10 AM. BTC is held as a cornerstone — never auto-sold.
